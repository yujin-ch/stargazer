Privacy

Last updated November 27, 2026.

Stargazer (playstargazer.io) is a small independent game. This page explains what information is involved when you play, in plain words. You can play without an account, and we never ask for your name, email or any other personal details to play. Signing in is optional and only used to keep your progress.

What stays on your device

Your observing log (scores and what you named), your streak, your catalogue of finds, and your settings (sound, night sounds, vibration) are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and we can't see them. Clearing your browser's site data for playstargazer.io deletes them.

If you sign in (optional)

You can sign in with Google or with a link sent to your email, so your observing log, universe and settings follow you to other devices. Sign-in is handled by our provider, Supabase. We then store your email address, an account id, and a copy of the same progress your browser keeps (scores, what you named, your settings). It is only used to restore your progress, is never sold or used for advertising, and each account can only read its own copy.

You can delete your saved progress at any time from Settings → Your account. To have your account removed entirely, email us from the address you signed in with.

What we don't collect

Unless you sign in, Stargazer doesn't send your scores or answers to any server, and it never shares them with other players. "On the hill tonight" is a curve worked out in your browser from tonight's sky, the same for every player, not a record of other people's scores.

Visit statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It doesn't use cookies, and visitors are counted without being identified across sites. Our host, Cloudflare, also keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

Advertising

Stargazer may show advertising provided by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Community skies

If you make a community sky, we store what you send (its title, description, the name you chose to show, your pictures, answers and notes, and its spot on the atlas) linked to your account, so you can delete it later. Everything except your account stays visible to other players once the sky is approved. Your browser redraws each picture before upload, which removes hidden data such as camera, date and location. When you report a sky, we store your reason. Deleting a sky removes its pictures and answers. See the community rules.

Children

Stargazer is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your device's own share sheet opens with your score, a picture of your night and a link. Nothing is posted anywhere unless you choose to send it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions or requests: hello@playstargazer.io.
