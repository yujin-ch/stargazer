Privacy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

Stargazer (playstargazer.io) is a small independent game. This page explains what information is involved when you play, in plain words. There are no accounts and we never ask for your name, email or any other personal details to play.

What stays on your device

Your observing log (scores and what you named), your streak, your catalogue of finds, and your settings (sound, night sounds, vibration) are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and we can't see them. Clearing your browser's site data for playstargazer.io deletes them.

What we don't collect

Stargazer doesn't send your scores or answers to any server. "On the hill tonight" is a curve worked out in your browser from tonight's sky, the same for every player, not a record of other people's scores.

Visit statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It doesn't use cookies, and visitors are counted without being identified across sites. Our host, Vercel, also keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

Advertising

Stargazer may show advertising provided by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Children

Stargazer is a general-audience game and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your device's own share sheet opens with your score, a picture of your night and a link. Nothing is posted anywhere unless you choose to send it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions or requests: hello@playstargazer.io.
