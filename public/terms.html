Community rules

Last updated November 27, 2026.

Community skies are skies made by players from their own pictures, placed on the Star Atlas for anyone to play. These are the rules for making one. By sending a sky you agree to them.

Who can make a sky

You need to be 13 or older and signed in. Playing community skies never needs an account.

Your pictures

  • Use photos you took yourself, or AI images you made yourself. Don't use pictures found online or taken by someone else.
  • No photos of people who haven't agreed to it, and nothing that shows where someone lives, their face or their personal details without their say.
  • Nothing sexual, violent, hateful, or cruel, nothing that harasses anyone, and nothing you wouldn't show a 13-year-old.
  • No ads, links or self-promotion in titles, names, notes or pictures.

Before anything is uploaded, your browser redraws each picture, which strips its hidden data (camera, date and location).

Review and reports

Every sky is checked by hand before anyone else can see it. We may turn a sky down, or take it off the atlas later, if it breaks these rules or doesn't work as a game. Any player can report a sky; after a few reports it is hidden until we look at it again.

What you let us do

You keep the rights to your pictures. By sending a sky you let Stargazer store it and show it to players in the game, in its share cards, and on the atlas, for as long as the sky is up. If you tick the box to let us feature your objects, you also let us use them in Stargazer's own daily or special skies, with credit to the name you gave.

Deleting your sky

You can delete your sky at any time from Community skies → Your skies. Its pictures and answers are removed for good. Skies can't be edited once sent.

Takedowns and contact

If a sky uses your picture or shows you without your consent, or you think it breaks these rules, use "Report this sky" after playing it, or write to hello@playstargazer.io with the sky's title. We'll look at it quickly and remove it if needed.
